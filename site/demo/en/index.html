How the costs are calculated

  • Staying costs five times the annual Microsoft licence cost and five times the annual cost of other existing IT. The three change costs are zero.
  • In each exit scenario, subtract the months of parallel running from 60. Multiply the annual cost of retained Microsoft licences by the remaining months and divide by 12.
  • Multiply the monthly hosting cost per user by the number of users and by 60. Hosting starts in the first month. Multiply each annual cost for operations, other target costs and other existing IT by five.
  • Direct project costs add one-time migration, application adaptation, external advice and training. Training costs are the days per person times the cost per person per day times the number of people to train.
  • The productivity loss block uses the amount entered for productivity loss.
  • Parallel running costs the full annual Microsoft licence amount times the number of parallel months, divided by 12.
  • The five-year total adds running costs and the three change costs. The comparison subtracts the total for staying from each scenario's total.
  • A line with a zero factor costs zero. A missing input needed by a calculation leaves the result unknown.
