Hosting

This site is hosted by BunnyWay d.o.o., Dunajska cesta 165, Ljubljana, Slovenia (bunny.net). The site’s files are stored in Frankfurt am Main and delivered only from data centres in Europe. When you open a page, Bunny’s servers receive your IP address and technical request details, such as the requested address and browser information. They use this data to deliver the page and protect the site from attacks. Bunny shortens IP addresses in its logs and deletes the logs after three days. We have a data processing agreement with Bunny under Article 28 GDPR. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in operating a secure and functional site.
