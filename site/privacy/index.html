Hosting

When you open a page, the web server receives your IP address and technical request details, such as the requested address and browser information. The server uses this data to deliver the page and operate the site securely. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in operating a secure and functional site.

Storage in your browser

If you change the colour mode, the site saves your choice only in your browser’s local storage under fazit-theme. This lets the browser restore the mode you selected when you return (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). Clearing this site’s stored data removes that choice.

Local assessment reports

Fazit creates the report on your computer. It includes the organisation name and the Windows-only application names you enter in inputs.yaml.

Contact

If you email or call, we use your message and contact details to respond. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for contract-related enquiries and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for our legitimate interest in answering other enquiries. We delete the data when it is no longer needed, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Links to other sites

If you follow a link to another site, its privacy policy applies there.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access (Article 15), rectification (Article 16), erasure (Article 17), restriction of processing (Article 18) and data portability (Article 20). You can object to processing (Article 21) and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Article 77), for example the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.
