Three free pilot assessments

Fazit is offering three free assessments for organisations with 200 to 2,000 staff, one each in Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

Expressions of interest are open until 28 February 2027 or until all three places are filled.

Express interest

What you receive

We help you run Fazit on a computer in your organisation. You keep the resulting HTML report. It covers dependencies, readiness across 15 areas, open source alternatives and a five-year cost comparison based on figures you supply.

You can review the sample report today. Assessments with your own data will start once local import of admin-centre exports is available.

Taking part

  1. Name a contact for the pilot.
  2. Run Fazit on a computer in your organisation.
  3. Join one feedback session about the tool and report.

Public bodies receive a one-page agreement for their approval process.

Data and publicity

We never ask for your assessment data, exclusivity or a commitment to buy or migrate. We also never ask for a logo, coat of arms, quote or badge on your website.

If you want us to name your organisation publicly, you can agree to it separately, in writing, later. You can withdraw it at any time.

Contact

To express interest, email hello@rouxdutoit.com with your country and approximate staff count.
