claim: The Office 365 E1 SKU is listed as bundling the Intune service plan, which makes the rule file report Intune as included in an Office 365 E1 licence.
evidence: "      - sku_part_number: \"STANDARDPACK\""
"      sku_guid: 18181a46-0d4e-45cd-891e-60aabd171b4e"
"      bundled:"
"        - INTUNE_O365"
why: STANDARDPACK is the Office 365 E1 SKU, and the comment labels it “Office 365 E1”. Intune is not part of Office 365 E1; it is part of Microsoft 365 E1 and other Microsoft 365 licences. A tenant with Office 365 E1 licences will therefore be told it has Intune entitlement it does not have.
