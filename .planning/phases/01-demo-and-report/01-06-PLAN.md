    `costs.py`. Constants `YEARS = 5`, `MONTHS = 60`, `MONTHS_PER_YEAR = 12`, `SCENARIOS = ("stay", "partial_exit", "full_exit")` and `BLOCKS = ("direct_project", "productivity_loss", "parallel_running")`. Frozen dataclasses `CostLine` (`id`, `amount`, `missing`), `CostGroup` (`id`, `lines`, `total`, `missing`) and `ScenarioResult` (`id`, `running`, `blocks`, `total`, `missing_inputs`, `difference_to_stay`). One public function, `compute_scenarios(inputs: Inputs) -> tuple[ScenarioResult, ...]`. It reads the `Inputs` dataclass and nothing else: no finding, no file, no clock. All arithmetic is in `decimal.Decimal`; nothing is rounded inside this module. The file holds no amount and no price.

    Lines follow the table in SKELETON.md section 8, with the letters defined there.
    - Staying: running cost has the lines `microsoft_licences` (M times 5) and `other_it` (O times 5); the three blocks are zero.
    - Partial exit and full exit: running cost has `retained_licences` (R times the months left after parallel running, divided by 12), `hosting` (H times U times 60: hosting is paid from the first month), `operations` (Op times 5), `other_recurring` (Q times 5) and `other_it` (O times 5). Block `direct_project` has `one_time_migration`, `application_adaptation`, `external_advice` and `training` (days per person times day rate times staff). Block `productivity_loss` has the one line of that name. Block `parallel_running` has one line: M times P divided by 12, because the full current licences are paid while both systems run.
    A product is zero when one of its factors is known to be zero, whatever the others are. Otherwise a line whose formula touches a null input has the amount `None` and lists the dotted `inputs.yaml` paths that are missing. A group's total is the sum of its lines, or `None` when one line is `None`. A scenario's total is the sum of running cost and the three blocks, or `None` when one of them is `None`; `missing_inputs` holds each missing path once, in formula order. `difference_to_stay` is the scenario total minus the total of staying, and `None` whenever either is unknown. Nothing is estimated or filled in (D-22).
