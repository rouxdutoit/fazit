## Part A: evidence and judgement

The principle behind all of part A: **missing, skipped, partial or unverifiable evidence must never produce Ready, and a confirmed problem must never be hidden.**

1. **A02.** A supplied answer must not erase a skipped observation. When a collector the finding belongs to was skipped, keep that observation in evaluation: the workload cannot be Ready, and the skip is listed with its reason. Test the reviewer's two-run case.
2. **A03.** Bind evidence to its source. In `rules/findings.yaml`, give each measurable finding the collectors and source codes allowed to produce it; the loader rejects any other pairing as an input error naming the finding. Shared mailbox counts may come only from the Exchange recipient export collector. Test the reviewer's case.
3. **A04, A07, H5.** Licence classification:
   - An assigned product with an empty or missing plan list makes the licence signal of every workload unknown for that product ("plan list missing"), never a measured absence.
   - One observed assignment of an add-on establishes presence even when overall licence coverage is partial; absence stays unknown under partial coverage, and the coverage warning remains.
   - An unknown service plan inside a known product affects only that plan: classify it as "not classified" and leave other workloads' signals intact. The all-workloads fallback applies only to an unknown product.
   - For the suites the samples use (Microsoft 365 E3, E5, Business Premium, Business Standard, F3, Office 365 E1, E3, and their EEA "no Teams" variants), list every service plan from Microsoft's licensing reference, with an explicit `no_workload` class for plans that do not matter here. Record the source URL and check date as before. Remove the US government, DoD, education and Telstra rows unless a test needs them.
   - Give both samples complete, real plan lists for their products, and add tests: one unlisted plan in a known product, one known product with an empty plan list, one add-on under partial coverage.
4. **A05.** Rules that require a provided confirmation evaluate against the provided observation, even when a measured observation exists and is preferred for display. If the two disagree, show both and treat it as a conflict (no Ready). Test the reviewer's case.
5. **A06, M2.** Any supplied total below a measured lower bound is a conflict: the measured lower bound decides, both values are shown with a conflict note, and the workload cannot be Ready. Lower bounds are never rounded up in display: show exact counts, and round byte sizes down. Test both reviewers' cases and the 1,250-byte case.
6. **A14.** Origin of answers must survive a round trip. Record in `inputs.yaml` whether answers are sample answers, independently of `amounts_are_invented`; `demo` writes that flag into its inputs copy, and `report` reads it. An unchanged round trip keeps "Sample input"; an organisation's own file says "Provided". Update the test that expects the change.
7. **M4.** Windows-only software without a web version is **Needs work** for a Microsoft 365 exit, with the reason that it blocks moving desktops away from Windows, not leaving Microsoft 365. Give the two desktop mapping rows a `need_finding`, so "Needed by this organisation" is answered from the Windows software answers.
8. **A15, L2.** Wording:
   - The English heading "Cannot be moved" becomes "Direct transfer unverified", matching the German "Nicht direkt übertragbar" and the body text.
   - Interview questions ask whether something is in use, in both languages, never whether someone can confirm it. Check every question in both checklists and in the locale strings, and make the English and German say the same thing.

## Part B: hardening and the public surface

1. **A01, M3, A10.** Identifier privacy:
   - Product and plan names shown in the report come only from `rules/service_plans.yaml`. An unregistered SKU or plan is shown as "Unregistered product 1", "Unregistered product 2" with a count, and is not copied verbatim into the JSON copy.
   - Unknown keys in `inputs.yaml` are reported by position, like the JSON reader, never echoed.
   - Control characters in printed file paths are escaped.
   - Extend `test_privacy.py` with single-token markers (`ReviewMarker`, `Erika_Mustermann`) in every identifier field and as an unknown YAML key, checked in HTML, JSON, file names, stdout and stderr.
