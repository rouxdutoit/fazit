## Phase Goal

Anyone can run `demo` and `report` and get an honest, self-contained report in German or English, with per-workload status, evidence on every finding and three cost scenarios, without a Microsoft account, without network access and before any Microsoft code exists.

> This is the ROADMAP.md goal line, copied as written. It is not in user-story form. No story was invented for it. `/gsd-mvp-phase 1` sets one.

<objective>
Show what moves where: three target profiles, mapped by function, each row with a statement of how well the target covers it and the date it was checked, plus the list of what cannot be moved and a suggested path that gives no verdict.

Purpose: A reader sees for each function what replaces it, how good the cover is, and on what evidence. Mapping content is its own plan because every row has to be checked against a project's documentation.
Output: Three mapping files, `load_mapping`, the sections `moves` and `path`, the sub-section "Cannot be moved", the mapping in the appendix, and `--profile` on both commands.

This is plan 4 of 7 (D-39).
</objective>
