# Plan 01-10: internal consistency of claimed completeness

Source: `01-CONFIRM-2.md` (second gate on HEAD `eb4e925`). Read it first: it confirms A02, A03, the Teams Phone regression and H5 resolved, and gives two new reproductions. Write `01-10-SUMMARY.md` mapping each item to the change and its test.

The rule from plan 01-09 stands: absence may only be concluded from complete, correctly sourced, fully classified evidence. This plan makes "complete" checkable against the data itself, so that a claim of completeness that the listed items contradict can never support a conclusion of absence.

1. **Missing product.** When the licence block claims complete coverage, `scanned` and `total` must equal the number of products listed, and `failed` must be zero. Any mismatch is an input error naming the field (exit 2). Test the gate's reproduction (claims 3 of 3, lists 2).
2. **Missing plan in a known product.** When a listed SKU is in the catalogue's roster, its plan list must contain every plan the roster gives for that SKU (matched by GUID). If any roster plan is missing, the product's plan list is incomplete: absence of add-ons is unknown for every workload, with the reason "plan list incomplete for a known product", exactly as for an unclassified plan. Extra plans not in the roster are treated as unclassified, as now. Test the gate's reproduction (`BUSINESS_VOICE_DIRECTROUTING` with `MCOEV` deleted) and one variant on another roster SKU.
3. **Look for the same pattern elsewhere.** Search the model and rules for any other place where a coverage claim or a count in the scan could disagree with the items the scan lists, and where that disagreement could turn into a conclusion of absence or Ready. Close each one the same way (input error when the scan contradicts itself; unknown when it is merely incomplete) with a test, and list in the summary every place you checked, including those that needed no change.
