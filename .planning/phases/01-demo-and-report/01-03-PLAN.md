## Phase Goal

Anyone can run `demo` and `report` and get an honest, self-contained report in German or English, with per-workload status, evidence on every finding and three cost scenarios, without a Microsoft account, without network access and before any Microsoft code exists.

> This is the ROADMAP.md goal line, copied as written. It is not in user-story form. No story was invented for it. `/gsd-mvp-phase 1` sets one.

<objective>
Add the judgement: a status for each of the fifteen workloads, decided from rules in data files, with missing evidence always shown as Unknown together with its reason and its fix.

Purpose: The report becomes a decision document that cannot make an organisation look more ready than the evidence allows. Everything that judges lives in YAML a consultant can edit.
Output: `rules.py`, three rule data files, the products table, the readiness section with its "Not examined" list, the risky-file part of "Needs attention", and the appendix entries for the rules.

This is plan 3 of 7 (D-39). Mappings and the suggested path are plan 01-04. Until plan 01-05 adds `inputs.yaml`, the samples lack the answers only a person can give, so most workloads of the demo read Unknown with the fix "answer this in inputs.yaml". That is the honest state, and the tests here build their own evidence.
</objective>
