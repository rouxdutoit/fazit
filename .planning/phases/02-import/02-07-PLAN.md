# Plan 02-07: licence classification over the whole roster

## Goal

A real tenant's licences are classified by area whatever products it holds, not only the 54 products somebody listed by hand. The hand-made entries keep their exact meaning, so nothing in the demo changes; every other product gets its add-on or bundled signal from one rule anyone can read.

<objective>
Built on the report redesign merged into main (D-91) and on plan 02-04's roster. Task 1 checks both and stops if either is missing.

Task 1 makes every roster identifier a registered code. Task 2 builds the service-plan classification over the roster with the derived signal and records the rule. Task 3 proves the rule against the hand data, the behaviour for unclassified plans and the unchanged demo.

Purpose: IMPT-06 ("only the classification is maintained by hand") and D-123. The plans that still have no classification entry (634 on the 2026-11-22 copy; whatever the supplied roster leaves) are classified by hand in plans 02-08 and 02-09; until then their products give `licence_not_classified`, which is the honest outcome.
Output: catalogue and licence-loader changes, one rule line in `service_plans.yaml`, a decision entry, tests, regenerated site demo reports (checksum lines only).

Run order of the phase: 02-00 and 02-01 (wave 1); 02-02 (wave 2); 02-03 and 02-04 (wave 3); 02-05 (wave 4); 02-06 and 02-07 (wave 5); then one plan per wave: 02-08, 02-09, 02-10, 02-11, 02-12, 02-13, 02-14 (waves 6 to 12).
