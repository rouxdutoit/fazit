## Phase Goal

The report shows an organisation's dependency on Microsoft's AI assistants and tells it when it has to decide before its licences renew.

Add the decision window exactly as D-90 decides: the licence contract's end date and notice period in `inputs.yaml`, shown in the report as the next renewal and the latest date to give notice, both marked as provided, or a sentence saying what to supply.

</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `inputs.yaml` → decision window | The contract dates are typed by a person and printed as provided facts |
| decision window → the reader's decision | A wrong or misread date can make an organisation miss its notice period |

## STRIDE Threat Register

Security level: ASVS 1. Blocking threshold: high.

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01.1-07 | Denial of service | `inputs.py` `licence_contract`: an out-of-range date or a huge notice period ends in a traceback | medium | mitigate | Bounds, calendar errors and wrong types become one-sentence input errors naming the field; tests for each (Tasks 1 and 2) |
| T-01.1-08 | Repudiation | decision window: provided dates read as measured facts, or the notice date read as legal advice | medium | mitigate | Both dates carry the Provided label, source and answer date; one sentence states the calculation; no other arithmetic or advice (D-90) (Task 1) |
| T-01.1-09 | Information disclosure | `licence_contract` values | low | mitigate | Only dates and integers are accepted; the existing `@` rejection and privacy tests apply |
| T-01.1-SC | Tampering | package installs | high | mitigate | This plan installs nothing and adds no dependency |
</threat_model>
