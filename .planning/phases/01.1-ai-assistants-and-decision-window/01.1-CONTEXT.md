# Phase 1.1 context: AI assistants and decision window

Decided by the founder on 2026-11-22. Research: `.planning/research/copilot-area.md` (sources accessed 2026-11-22). These decisions are locked for planning; `01-CONTEXT.md` (D-01 to D-80) still applies.

## AI assistants (AIAS-01)

- **D-81:** The new area `copilot`, labelled "AI assistants (Copilot)" / "KI-Assistenten (Copilot)", is the second area everywhere: report, site area list and coverage table. It covers Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Business, Copilot Studio and Copilot Chat. When the phase ships, and not before, the site's figure tile changes to "16 areas assessed, from mail to AI agents" / "16 Bereiche, von E-Mail bis KI-Agenten", and every other "15 areas" / "fünfzehn Bereiche" on the site, in the READMEs and in the report becomes 16.
- **D-82:** Only paid Copilot add-on products prove deliberate purchase and count as measured evidence. Copilot included in a suite, including Microsoft 365 E7, is an entitlement and proves nothing about use, the same rule as other bundled plans. Classification keys on the product (SKU), not on its exact plan list, because Microsoft's CSV and reference page list different plans for the same add-on.
- **D-83:** Copilot Chat is not inferred from licences. `Bing_Chat_Enterprise` is classified `no_workload` for this area. Its use comes from the Copilot Chat usage report (phase 2 import) or a provided answer.
- **D-84:** The area is Ready only with a provided answer that Copilot is not in use, as for telephony. No evidence is Unknown, never Ready. Blocker: Copilot is used in processes that must keep running and no replacement is confirmed. Needs work: add-on licences, Copilot Studio capacity, agents, connected data sources or confirmed use.
- **D-85:** The Copilot Studio for Teams plans (`POWER_VIRTUAL_AGENTS_O365_*`) move from `power_platform` to `copilot`, because they build agents.
- **D-86:** No new cost formula. A replacement assistant, its hosting or API use, agent rework and training fit the existing `inputs.yaml` blocks; the interview checklist names them. Fazit never fills in prices.
- **D-87:** Importers for the Agents usage report and the Power Platform inventory wait for phase 2 and real exports, because Microsoft does not document their columns. Phase 1.1 uses the licence block and provided answers only.
- **D-88:** Mappings follow the research: Nextcloud's assistant, file answers and transcription are `in_suite_with_limits`; openDesk's AI in Collabora and Notes (operator-supplied endpoint, off by default) is `in_suite_with_limits`; custom agent builders are `not_available` in both; the product-neutral profile names self-hosted assistants without endorsing one. Every row has a source and the date 2026-11-22.
- **D-89:** Copilot Business identifiers are missing from Microsoft's reference. Do not invent them; record the gap in the catalogue notes and recheck at Microsoft's next update.
